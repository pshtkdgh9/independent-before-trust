## ACL backup check

- The latest published ACL main-conference CFP is ACL 2026. It required submission through the ARR October 2025 or January 2026 cycles, with a January 5, 2026 submission deadline and March 14, 2026 commitment deadline. Those windows have passed and ACL 2026 is not a viable target for new work.
- ACL 2026 explicitly included AI/LLM Agents, Dialogue and Interactive Systems, and Discourse, Pragmatics, and Reasoning, supporting the broader ACL-family fit of LAD. This is venue-fit evidence only; the expired call is not used as a scheduling assumption.
- The official ACL 2027 site announces Kyoto, Japan, August 17--22, 2027, but does not yet publish a main-paper CFP or submission/commitment deadlines as of the check date. ACL 2027 therefore remains an unscheduled backup. The project will re-check its official CFP before any commitment decision.

## Interpretation for this project

- EACL 2027 is the correct main target because it currently has a live CFP and a concrete ARR commitment window.
- ACL 2027 is not a schedulable backup yet because its website lists all deadlines as TBA.
- The paper must remain self-contained in the main PDF; supplementary files can support reproducibility but must not carry essential claims.
- The limitations section should be explicit and written as a required section, not hidden in the appendix.
- Any generative AI assistance used in drafting or coding must be disclosed.

## Practical submission constraints

- The final manuscript must be anonymous.
- The submission must be original and not overlap with prior submitted or published work.
- The responsible NLP checklist must be filled out carefully and tied to specific sections of the paper.
- The paper should be written so that it still makes sense if supplementary files are not read.
